# 🔧 Willistown Auto Repair — Website Project

> A cinematic, high-converting landing page built with [Lovable.dev](https://lovable.dev/) for a trusted local auto repair shop with 40+ years of service and a 4.9★ Google rating.

---

## 📸 Overview

This repo contains the **Lovable.dev prompt** (and resulting source code) for the Willistown Auto Repair website — a dark, luxury-aesthetic, single-page marketing site designed to convert first-time visitors into booked customers.

**The goal:** Make a family-owned auto shop look like the most trustworthy, premium mechanic in the region — because they are.

---

## ✨ Design Direction

| Property | Choice |
|---|---|
| **Aesthetic** | Dark, cinematic, luxury |
| **Primary Color** | `#0f0f0f` / `#1a1a1a` (near-black) |
| **Accent** | `#c9a84c` (warm gold) |
| **Typography** | Bold oversized sans-serif display font |
| **Vibe** | *"This isn't your sketchy strip-mall mechanic."* |

---

## 📄 Page Sections

1. **Hero** — Full-viewport dark cinematic intro with headline, subheadline, and dual CTAs
2. **Trust Bar** — 5 at-a-glance credibility stats (40+ years, 4.9★, PA inspection station, etc.)
3. **Why Willistown** — 3-column card section: Honest · Fast · Fair Priced
4. **Services Grid** — Icon tiles for all core offerings with gold hover effects
5. **Reviews** — 6 curated real Google reviews in a horizontal scroll/masonry layout
6. **Fleet & Commercial CTA** — Dedicated pitch for commercial/fleet accounts
7. **About / Story** — Brand narrative covering the shop's 40-year history
8. **Booking / Contact** — Lead capture form + address, hours, phone, Google Maps
9. **Footer** — Minimal: logo, nav, contact info, Google Reviews badge

---

## 🗣️ Key Selling Points (From Real Reviews)

These themes were pulled directly from 54 Google reviews and baked into the copy:

- ✅ **Radically honest** — tells you what *needs* fixing, not what pads the bill
- ✅ **Fast & responsive** — emergency same-day service, fits you in
- ✅ **Fair, transparent pricing** — no inflated estimates, no surprise charges
- ✅ **40+ year track record** — multi-generational customer loyalty
- ✅ **Fleet-capable** — services trucks, trailers, and commercial vehicles
- ✅ **PA State Inspection Station**

## 🚀 How to Use the Prompt

1. Go to [lovable.dev](https://lovable.dev/) and start a new project
2. Paste the full prompt from [`PROMPT.md`](./PROMPT.md) into the chat
3. If the vibe isn't dark/gold enough on first pass, follow up with:
   > *"Make it darker and more cinematic — less gray, more near-black with warm gold accents throughout."*
4. Iterate from there — Lovable's chat is your design director
